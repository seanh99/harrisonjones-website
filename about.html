About

Harrison Jones works with architecture studios as Main Contractor and Project Manager, delivering projects from planning through to completion.

The company coordinates contractors, manages programme and cost, and ensures each project is executed in line with the design intent set by the architect. Studios retain full authorship of the design; Harrison Jones focuses on delivery.

Founded by Clifford Jones, the company draws on more than forty years of experience across residential and commercial projects where detail matters — from complex refurbishments to high-end new builds, in the UK and internationally. Sean Harrison brings a complementary, technically grounded approach to the practice, with a decade spent leading architecture and BIM-driven technical delivery across Europe, the Americas and the Caribbean.

Harrison Jones operates as a single point of responsibility for delivery — coordinating contractors, consultants and programme so that a project keeps moving as one continuous process, rather than passing between disconnected teams.

Projects are undertaken internationally, for architecture studios and their clients who value a practical, detail-driven partner to see a project through construction to completion.
